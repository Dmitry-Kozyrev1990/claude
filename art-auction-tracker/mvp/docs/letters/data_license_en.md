# Letter to a price database owner: data licence request

**Subject:** Data licence for auction results — art analytics service

Dear [name],

My name is [name], founder of Lotline, an art market analytics and valuation service for
collectors and banks. We would like to license your auction results data. We respect your
database rights, which is why we are asking for an agreement rather than collecting data
from your website.

**What we are looking for**
- Auction results for [segments: Russian art, prints and multiples, …] from [years].
- Fields: auction house, sale date, lot, artist, title, year, medium, dimensions, estimate,
  price, status; images or image links where possible.
- Regular updates.

**How we would use it**
- Price indices, market statistics and fair-value estimates in our service.
- Lot-level results shown to our users with your database credited as the source.
- [If relevant] a B2B API for banks and insurers.

**Open to discussing**
- A fixed fee, revenue share, or an exchange: our cross-house artwork matching data and our
  alerts service.
- A non-exclusive licence for [N] years with renewal.

I would be grateful for a contact for data licensing.

Kind regards,
[name, title, phone, email]
